# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want selfcheck to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their
experience, background or identity. We pledge to act and interact in ways that
contribute to an open, friendly and healthy community.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- being kind and patient, and assuming good intent;
- giving and taking feedback on the work, not the person — the same standard
  selfcheck asks of its own review notes;
- owning mistakes and learning from them;
- focusing on what is best for the people who use the project.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks;
- sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind;
- publishing someone's private information without their permission;
- anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that break this code, and
may ban contributors who do so repeatedly or seriously. Report problems to the
maintainers privately through
[a private GitHub advisory](https://github.com/Unchained-Labs/selfcheck/security/advisories/new).
Reports are handled in confidence.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions —
and when someone represents the project in public.
